### Experience

- **AI Hub Assistant**, Center for Innovation in Teaching and Learning, American University of Sharjah (since November 2024). I led the redesign of the AI Hub website, [aihub.aus.edu](https://aihub.aus.edu/), and write evaluations of new AI tools for faculty.
- **Web Design and Development Consultant**, College of Engineering, American University of Sharjah (since August 2026).
- **Account Manager**, Chief Nest, remote (since September 2025).
- **President**, Tau Beta Pi UAE Alpha, the engineering honor society's chapter at AUS (previously Vice President).
- Previously AI/ML Engineer at Samsung Gulf Electronics, Dubai (June to August 2026). I architected and solely built a production trend intelligence platform for the regional marketing team.
- Previously Undergraduate Research Assistant at AUS (October 2024 to July 2025): speech emotion recognition from log-mel spectrograms with fine-tuned convolutional networks, at roughly 75% accuracy.

### Projects

Full case studies are at **[ahmadsaad.dev/projects](https://ahmadsaad.dev/projects)**.

- **[Trend Radar](https://github.com/ahm-adsaad/trend-radar)**: a production TikTok trend detection platform where measurement decides and LLMs describe. [Case study](https://ahmadsaad.dev/projects/trend-radar)
- **[LocalAI](https://github.com/ahm-adsaad/LocalAI)**: private AI for company documents that runs entirely in the browser on WebGPU, so no document ever leaves the device. [Live demo](https://localai.ahmadsaad.dev) · [Case study](https://ahmadsaad.dev/projects/localai)
- **[Mano Basic Computer Simulator](https://github.com/ahm-adsaad/manos-basic-computer-simulator)**: a cycle-accurate simulator of Mano's Basic Computer in Python, with full ISA support and a CLI debugger. [Case study](https://ahmadsaad.dev/projects/mano-computer-simulator)
- **LoRaWAN sensor node**: a custom 2-layer PCB with an ATmega4809 MCU, a LoRa radio and an energy harvesting power path. [Case study](https://ahmadsaad.dev/projects/lorawan-sensor-node)
